# Aurevia Jewels Image Rights Review

Date verified: 2026-08-31

This document is an engineering and asset compliance record, not legal advice.

## Official licensing basis

### Pexels

- Official license page: [Pexels Free Stock Photo & Video License](https://www.pexels.com/legal-pages/license/)
- Help article: [Can I use the photos and videos for a commercial project?](https://help.pexels.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042295214-Can-I-use-the-photos-and-videos-for-a-commercial-project)
- Commercial use: allowed for websites, products, marketing, and related business uses
- Attribution: not required, though appreciated
- Key limitation: depicted people, brands, trademarks, logos, artwork, and property rights may still require separate review; endorsement must not be implied

### Unsplash

- Official license page: [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license)
- Help article: [Can I use Unsplash images for personal or commercial projects?](https://help.unsplash.com/en/articles/2612315-can-i-use-unsplash-images-for-personal-or-commercial-projects)
- Attribution: not required by the core license, but appreciated
- Commercial use: allowed for most commercial and editorial uses
- Key limitation: the license does not remove third-party rights concerns such as publicity, privacy, trademarks, logos, or artwork

## Launch interpretation

- The storefront currently uses self-hosted stock photography only.
- No runtime dependency on `images.unsplash.com` or `images.pexels.com` remains.
- Commercial website use is generally permitted by the source licenses for the current stock assets.
- Stock licensing is not the same thing as ownership of the photographed item.
- For a real commercial storefront, product pages should ideally use owner-supplied product photography of the exact inventory shipped to customers.
